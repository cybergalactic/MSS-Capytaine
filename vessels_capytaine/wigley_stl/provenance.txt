Preparation:
- Reversed the source normals.
- Clipped the hull at source z = 0.
- Simplified the positive-y half to 900 faces using fast-simplification 0.2.0.
- Mirrored the half hull for exact port/starboard symmetry.
- Pinned the free boundary to the waterline.
- Converted to FSD coordinates using (x, -y, -z) and scaled uniformly by 20.
- Saved as binary STL.
